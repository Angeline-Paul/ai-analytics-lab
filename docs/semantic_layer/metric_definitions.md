# Metric Definitions

## total_revenue

Business Definition:
Total revenue generated from all orders.

Calculation:
SUM(amount)

Source Model:
fct_revenue

---

## lifetime_value

Business Definition:
Total historical revenue generated by a customer.

Calculation:
SUM(amount)

Source Model:
fct_customer_kpis

---

## avg_order_value

Business Definition:
Average value of a customer order.

Calculation:
AVG(amount)

Source Model:
fct_customer_kpis
